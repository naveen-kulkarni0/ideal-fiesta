Your Visit

What to Expect

A clear, step-by-step journey from your first consultation to long-term maintenance

01

Book & Prepare

Schedule online or call us. Bring any previous X-rays, medical history, and a list of medications. We'll send intake forms digitally to save time.

02

Comprehensive Consultation

60-minute visit: clinical exam, CBCT scan, intraoral scan, photos, and discussion of your goals. No pressure — just thorough understanding.

03

Personalized Treatment Plan

Receive a detailed digital plan with options, timeline, and transparent fees. We explain every step. You decide what's right for you.

04

Precise Treatment

Digital workflows, guided surgery, and in-house fabrication for predictable results. Comfort options include topical anesthetic and sedation if needed.

05

Review & Maintain

Post-treatment review at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months. Then customized recall schedule. Your long-term success is our responsibility.
